01 / The challenge

The cost of deciding on belief.

Most growth decisions get made on belief. Someone in the room is sure the market wants it, everyone agrees, the money goes out, and eighteen months later the thing that was obvious in the boardroom turns out to be wrong in the market.

The cost isn't the build. It's the eighteen months.

The Growth Opportunity Sprint exists to settle that before you spend. One opportunity, ten days, evidence gathered from buyers rather than from inside your own company.

02 / Your outcome

What you leave with.

Not another deck. The evidence and business case you need to make a decision.

03 / How it works

Ten days from assumption to decision.

A fixed sequence designed to take a single growth opportunity from assumptions to evidence and a defensible business case.

Days 01–02

Define what must be true.

We pick the opportunity and define what would have to be true for it to work. You get a written brief of the assumptions we're testing before we test anything.

Days 03–06

Go to the market.

Customer and buyer conversations, competitor and substitute analysis, and whatever data you already hold, used properly for once.

Days 07–08

Test the assumptions.

We test the assumptions against what came back. Some of them break, which is the point. What survives is your evidence.

Days 09–10

Build the business case.

The opportunity, the numbers, the cost to pursue, the case against, and the recommendation. One page, plus the evidence behind it.

A decision you can defend — including when the evidence says stop.

04 / Fit matters

The right fit. Not everyone.

✓ Who this is for

  • Owners and founders who know the growth move they want and can't yet prove it's the right one.
  • Teams who need a business case before committing to a build.
  • Businesses where the last pilot worked and then died before it scaled.
  • Anyone who has been asked to justify a growth bet and doesn't have the evidence to do it.

− Who this isn't for

  • Teams who already have the evidence. You don't need this.
  • Anyone looking for a strategy document. This produces a decision, not a deck.
  • Businesses that can't give us access to a handful of customers or buyers in the ten days.

06 / Your questions

Straight answers.

What to expect, what we need from you, and why a no-go is a valuable result.

Is this consulting?+

It's a sprint with a fixed output. You're not buying our time by the hour, you're buying a business case in ten days.

What do you need from us?+

One decision-maker who owns the opportunity, access to a handful of your customers or buyers, and two hours of your time across the ten days.

What if the evidence says don't do it?+

Then you've saved the cost of the build. That's a result, and it's the most common one.

Three questions: what the growth number is and by when, what you've already tried to prove it, and who else has to agree before the money goes out. If the sprint fits, we offer you a January start on the call.
